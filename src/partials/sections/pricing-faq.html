Questions we hear most often.

  • How does it work?

    Every plan is a monthly retainer of specialist hours. Brief us, and we match you with vetted people.

  • Who are the specialists?

    Vetted remote developers, designers and support specialists. You meet them on a call before work begins.

  • What about time zones?

    We agree overlap hours at the start. Your specialists join your chat and calls and send a weekly update.

  • Can I switch or pause?

    Yes. On a monthly plan you can move up, move down or pause from your next billing date.

  • What counts as an hour?

    Time a specialist spends on your work: tasks, reviews and meetings. Hours are logged per task and shared weekly.

    Send the form below. We reply within one business day, agree the scope and introduce your specialists.
